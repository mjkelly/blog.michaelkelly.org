I've been spending most of my waking hours at [The Next HOPE](http://thenexthope.org/) the last two days. Big notes to self (and anyone who reads this) so far:  

*   Support [Tor](http://www.torproject.org/). I've got an always-on bastion machine on my little apartment network. Maybe I should run a Tor node?
*   Check out [Monkeysphere](http://web.monkeysphere.info/). Use an OpenPGP web of trust to authenticate SSH and SSL keys! There's no Chromium plugin as yet. Consider helping with that?
*   Firefox plugins: [HTTPS Everywhere](https://www.eff.org/https-everywhere), [Perspectives](http://www.cs.cmu.edu/%7Eperspectives/firefox.html), [Certificate Patrol](http://patrol.psyced.org/).
*   [Club-Mate](https://secure.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/wiki/Club-Mate) is very nice.
*   USB tethering works extremely well with the Nexus One and a Linux laptop (assuming you've got good cell coverage), and tethering over GSM can be a nice alternative to a wifi network you don't really trust.
