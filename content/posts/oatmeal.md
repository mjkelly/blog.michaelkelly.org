There is a very very good way to make oatmeal that is different from what's on
the box. This results in a hearty, porridge-y breakfast. The key parts are
using more than twice called-for amount of water, and adding ground flaxseed.

Once it's done, it's a great base to add spices or other flavorings to.

This makes 2 servings.

## Ingredients
1. 1 cup of 1-minute oats (Quaker Oats, Bob's Red Mill, Whole Foods, etc).
2. 4-5 cups of water
3. 1 pinch of salt
4. 2 tbsp ground flaxseed

## Instructions
Boil the 4 cups of water in a small pot, with the lid on. Add salt while
heating the water.

Once water is boiling, take lid off and add the oatmeal. Keep the heat high
right after you add the oatmeal, but don't walk away. Wait for the water to
start boiling again, then reduce to a simmer.

Find the equilibrium point for your pot, stove, and oatmeal: you want the heat
as high as possible, while not creating foam at the top which will boil over.

The oats will float at the top initially, and it is as this point where
everything is the most likely to boil over.

Once the oats settle to the bottom of the pot, stir them and raise the heat a
bit. Again, find the equilibrium point. For me, it is about 25%-50% higher than
the first equilibrium.

Keep boiling until the oats have absorbed all the water. You're looking for one
consistent mixture. You shouldn't see oats floating in water, and you shouldn't
see any foam at the top. It'll still be a bit lumpy. Bubbles should come up
with a soft "pop", the way you might imagine lava.

Stir every 30s-1m to scrape oats off the bottom to keep them from sticking and
burning.

Once oats are done, turn off the heat and add the 2tbsp of ground flaxseed. Mix
it in, and serve.

If you like milk (or soy/almond/oat milk), you can add it now as well.

## Serving

Add whatever flavorings you like. Some ideas are:
* Soy milk or oat milk
* A little bit of coffee creamer
* Cinnamon and nutmeg
* Honey
* Fresh blueberries
* Raw almonds or raw walnuts
* Seeds (like shelled sunflower seeds)
* Peanut butter (if you're feeling chaotic)
